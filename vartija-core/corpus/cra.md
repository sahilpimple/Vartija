## [cra:art14-1] CRA Article 14(1): reporting actively exploited vulnerabilities
From 11 September 2026, a manufacturer that becomes aware of an actively exploited vulnerability in its product with digital elements must notify it. One submission through ENISA's Single Reporting Platform reaches both the CSIRT designated as coordinator and ENISA.

## [cra:art14-2] CRA Article 14(2): timeline for exploited vulnerabilities
An early warning is due within 24 hours of becoming aware, naming where relevant the Member States in which the product is available. A vulnerability notification follows within 72 hours with general information about the product, the general nature of the exploit and vulnerability, corrective or mitigating measures taken, measures users can take, and how sensitive the information is. A final report follows no later than 14 days after a corrective or mitigating measure is available, describing the vulnerability with its severity and impact, any information about the malicious actor, and the security update or other fix.

## [cra:art14-3] CRA Article 14(3)-(4): severe incidents affecting product security
A manufacturer must also notify any severe incident that affects the security of its product, through the same platform. The early warning is due within 24 hours and says whether the incident is suspected to be caused by unlawful or malicious acts; an incident notification is due within 72 hours; and a final report within one month of that notification.

## [cra:art14-5] CRA Article 14(5): what makes an incident "severe"
An incident is severe when it negatively affects, or could affect, the product's ability to protect the availability, authenticity, integrity or confidentiality of sensitive or important data or functions, or when it has led, or could lead, to the introduction or execution of malicious code in the product or in users' systems.

## [cra:art14-8] CRA Article 14(8): informing users
After becoming aware of an actively exploited vulnerability or a severe incident, the manufacturer informs impacted users, and where appropriate all users, about it and about any risk-mitigation and corrective measures they can take.
