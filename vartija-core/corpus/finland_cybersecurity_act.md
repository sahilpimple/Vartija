## [fi-cyberact:reporting] Finland: incident reporting under the Cybersecurity Act
Finland implements NIS2 through the Cybersecurity Act (124/2025), applied from 8 April 2025. A covered entity sends an early warning within 24 hours of detecting a significant incident, an incident notification within 72 hours, and a final report within one month. Supervision is split by sector; Traficom's National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC-FI) acts as the single point of contact and coordinates the supervisory authorities.

## [fi-cyberact:management] Finland: responsibility of management
Under the Finnish act the entity's management is responsible for organising and overseeing cybersecurity risk management. Supply-chain risk, incident detection and handling, and staff training are part of the minimum measures.

## [fi-cyberact:supervisors] Finland: who supervises which sector
Sectoral authorities keep supervision of their own sectors (for example the Energy Authority for energy and the Financial Supervisory Authority for finance), while Traficom supervises communications and digital-infrastructure entities and coordinates the overall system.
